Chatbot. Script. Agent.

A customer disputes a charge. Three tools, three very different outcomes.

Chatbot

Works from a question
  • Explains the refund policy
  • Talks about surprises
  • Rarely acts in systems
  • Human does the follow-through

Automation (RPA)

Works from a fixed script
  • Refunds only if the case matches the rules
  • Breaks on surprises
  • Runs only the steps it was given
  • Human maintains the script

AI agent

Works from a goal
  • Checks the order and the policy, issues the refund
  • Reasons through many surprises
  • Chooses its own steps
  • Human sets the goal and handles exceptions

How an agent works

Better reasoning, standard ways to plug models into tools, and orchestration software that tracks long chains of steps made this possible. Tap a step.

Agents with no escalation path stall or answer wrongly with full confidence.

Where this leaves us

Agentic AI has moved past the lab-curiosity stage. In narrow, well-bounded jobs it delivers measurable results. But the gap between “we use agents” and “we trust agents” is still wide, and that gap, more than any model upgrade, will decide which projects reach 2027.
